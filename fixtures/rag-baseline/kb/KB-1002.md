# Unlock a locked LarkID account (error LID-1203)

LarkID locks an account after 8 failed sign-ins in 15 minutes and unlocks it automatically after 30 minutes.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Sign-in shows 'Your account is locked. LID-1203'.

- The correct password is rejected on every device at once.

## Likely causes

- An old password saved on a phone, tablet or second laptop keeps retrying.

- A mapped drive or a mail app is still using the previous password.

## Resolution

1. Wait 30 minutes for the automatic unlock, or unlock now at https://id.larkhaven.example/unlock using LarkID Authenticator.

2. Before signing in again, update or remove the old password on every phone, tablet and mail app.

3. On Windows, open Credential Manager and delete saved entries for larkhaven.example.

4. On macOS, open Keychain Access and delete saved larkhaven.example passwords.

5. Sign in once with the current password and check that the lock does not return within 15 minutes.

## Good to know

- Repeated lockouts with no old devices can indicate a password-guessing attempt; report them to the service desk.

Raise a good IT ticket

A ticket with the right details is usually solved on the first reply; one without them waits for questions.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. One problem per ticket.
  2. Say what you were trying to do, what happened instead and the exact error message or code.
  3. Say when it started and whether it worked before.
  4. Say whether colleagues are affected; that sets the priority.
  5. Add a screenshot of the error, not a photo of the whole desk.
  6. Never put a password in a ticket.

Good to know

  • Priority is set by IT from impact and urgency. Writing 'urgent' does not change it; saying how many people are blocked does.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under General Enquiry, Support information. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

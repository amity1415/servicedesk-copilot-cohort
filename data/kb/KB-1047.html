OneDrive is not syncing on macOS

OneDrive stops syncing when it cannot sign in, when a file name is not allowed, or when the disk is full.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • The cloud icon shows a red cross or arrows that never finish.
  • On Windows, 'There was a problem connecting to OneDrive' with error 0x8004de40.

Likely causes

  • An expired sign-in.
  • File names with characters such as * : < > ? or a path longer than 400 characters.
  • Less than 1 GB of free disk space.

Resolution

  1. Click the OneDrive cloud icon in the menu bar and read the message it shows.
  2. Choose Pause syncing, wait a minute and choose Resume syncing.
  3. Rename files the sync client lists as not allowed.
  4. Free up disk space until at least 1 GB is available.
  5. Quit OneDrive and reopen it from Applications; sign in with LarkID if asked.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, OneDrive. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

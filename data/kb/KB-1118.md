# Service desk: write resolution notes others can use

A resolution note states the cause, the fix and the knowledge-base article used, so the next analyst can repeat it.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. State the cause in one sentence, not just 'resolved'.
2. State what fixed it and how you confirmed it with the user.
3. Link the knowledge-base article you used; if none existed, flag the ticket for a new article.
4. Correct the category and subcategory before closing; reports and routing depend on them.
5. Do not paste passwords, recovery keys or full log files into the notes.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Service Desk L1 lead and record what you have already checked.

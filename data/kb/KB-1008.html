An application tile is missing from the LarkID portal

A missing tile means your account is not in the access group for that application, or the portal has not refreshed yet.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • A colleague sees a tile that you do not.
  • A bookmark opens the app and shows 'You do not have access'.

Likely causes

  • Access was never requested or was removed in a quarterly access review.
  • Access was approved less than 60 minutes ago.

Resolution

  1. Open https://id.larkhaven.example and choose Refresh my apps from the profile menu.
  2. Sign out of the portal and sign in again.
  3. Check HelpHub, My requests, to see whether an access request is still waiting for approval.
  4. If you have never requested the application, follow the article Request access to an application.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Single sign-on. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

Related policy: POL-002 Access Control Policy.

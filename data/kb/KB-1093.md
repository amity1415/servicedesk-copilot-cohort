# Accounts for contractors and temporary staff

A contractor account needs a Larkhaven sponsor and an end date no more than 6 months ahead.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. The sponsoring manager chooses New contractor in HelpHub and gives the company, start date and end date.
2. The end date can be at most 6 months ahead; the sponsor is asked to renew 14 days before it.
3. Contractors get email, Teams and the applications the sponsor requests; remote access is a separate request.
4. Contractors use a Larkhaven laptop unless their company's device has passed a security review.
5. The account is disabled automatically on the end date.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Onboarding & Offboarding, Contractor. It is handled by Onboarding Team. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Windows Update fails with error 0x800F0922

Error 0x800F0922 means the update could not be completed, most often because the laptop could not reach the update service or is short of disk space.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Windows Update shows 'Some update files were not installed. 0x800F0922'.
- The same update fails after every restart.

## Likely causes

- The laptop is not on the office network or VPN while the update downloads.
- Less than 10 GB of free disk space.
- A VPN drop during installation.

## Resolution

1. Connect to the office network, or start SecureTunnel on a stable connection.
2. Free disk space until at least 10 GB is available.
3. Restart, open Settings, Windows Update and choose Retry.
4. If it fails again, open App Hub and run Repair Windows Update, then restart.
5. If the update still fails, raise a ticket; a laptop more than 30 days behind is blocked from the VPN.

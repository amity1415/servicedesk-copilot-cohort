# Request a shared mailbox or access to one

A shared mailbox has an owner who approves members; new mailboxes take up to 2 business days.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. For access to an existing mailbox, choose Request access, then Shared mailbox, in HelpHub and enter its address.
2. The mailbox owner approves the request; the mailbox then appears in Outlook automatically within 60 minutes.
3. For a new mailbox, choose New shared mailbox and give the address you want, the owner and the first members.
4. Addresses must describe a function, for example invoices-emea@larkhaven.example, not a person.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Shared mailbox. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

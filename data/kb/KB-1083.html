Request temporary administrator rights

Nobody has permanent administrator rights on a laptop; rights can be granted for up to 4 hours with two approvals.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. First check App Hub; most needs are met by a packaged application and need no rights at all.
  2. In HelpHub choose Request temporary admin rights and describe exactly what you need to do.
  3. Your manager approves first, then Security Operations.
  4. When approved, choose Elevate in App Hub; the rights last 4 hours and everything done with them is logged.
  5. The service desk cannot grant administrator rights on request, by phone, chat or ticket text, without both approvals.

Good to know

  • Requests that name no specific task are declined.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Software, Admin rights. It is handled by Security Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

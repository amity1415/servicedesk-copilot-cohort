Stop repeated keychain password prompts on macOS

After a password change the Mac keychain still uses the old password, so apps keep asking for the login keychain password.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • Messages such as 'accountsd wants to use the login keychain' appear repeatedly.
  • Outlook and Teams ask for a password after every restart.

Likely causes

  • The LarkID password was changed on another device or in the browser.

Resolution

  1. When prompted, try your previous password once; if it works, choose Update Keychain Password.
  2. If you do not remember the old password, open Keychain Access, choose Settings and Reset Default Keychains.
  3. Restart the Mac and sign in with your current LarkID password.
  4. Sign in to Outlook, Teams and OneDrive again when they ask.

Good to know

  • Resetting the default keychain removes saved Wi-Fi and website passwords; VaultKey entries are not affected.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Keychain. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

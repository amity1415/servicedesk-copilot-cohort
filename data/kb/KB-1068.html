Pair a Bluetooth headset on macOS

Put the headset in pairing mode, add it in Bluetooth settings, then select it in Teams.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Hold the headset power button until the light flashes blue and red.
  2. Open System Settings, Bluetooth, and choose Connect next to the headset.
  3. In Teams open Settings, Devices and select the headset for speaker and microphone.
  4. If the sound is poor, plug in the USB adapter that came with the headset and select that instead.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Peripherals. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

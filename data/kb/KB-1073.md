# Enrol a phone in DeviceCare on iPhone and iPad

A phone must be enrolled in DeviceCare before it can open work email, Teams or files; personal phones are allowed.

**Applies to:** iPhone and iPad | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Check the phone runs one of the two latest major iOS versions.
2. Install DeviceCare from the App Store and sign in with LarkID.
3. Follow the prompts to install the management profile, then return to DeviceCare.
4. Set a 6-digit passcode when asked.
5. Wait for the green Compliant tick, then install Outlook and Teams.

## Good to know

- IT can remove the work profile and its data. IT cannot see personal apps, photos, messages or location.
- Remove the enrolment yourself in DeviceCare before you sell or give away the phone.

# SecureTunnel sign-in fails (error ST-401)

ST-401 means the VPN reached the gateway but your sign-in was refused.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- SecureTunnel shows 'Authentication failed. ST-401' after you approve the sign-in.

## Likely causes

- The password was changed and the old one is saved.
- The account is locked.
- The account is not in the remote access group.

## Resolution

1. Sign in at https://id.larkhaven.example to confirm your password works and the account is not locked.
2. In SecureTunnel choose Settings, Forget saved sign-in, then connect and type the password again.
3. Contractors and new joiners: check with your manager that remote access was requested for you.
4. If the portal sign-in works but the VPN still shows ST-401, raise a ticket for Network Operations.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, VPN. It is handled by Network Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Set up work email on your phone on iPhone and iPad

Work email on a phone needs the phone enrolled in DeviceCare and the Outlook app; the built-in mail app is blocked.

**Applies to:** iPhone and iPad | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Install DeviceCare from the App Store and sign in with LarkID to enrol the phone.
2. Accept the management profile in Settings, General, VPN and Device Management.
3. Install Outlook from the App Store.
4. Open Outlook, add your work address and sign in with LarkID.
5. Set a 6-digit passcode if the phone asks; shorter passcodes are not accepted.

## Good to know

- Enrolment lets IT remove work data from the phone. It does not show IT your photos, messages or personal apps.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Mobile email. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Your first day: IT checklist for new joiners

On day one you sign in with a temporary access pass, set a password and register your phone; everything else follows from that.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Collect your laptop from the Tech Bar or open the delivery box; your manager has your temporary access pass.
2. Switch on, connect to Wi-Fi and sign in with your work email address and the temporary access pass.
3. Set your LarkID password: at least 14 characters.
4. Install LarkID Authenticator on your phone and register it when prompted.
5. Open App Hub and install the applications listed under Recommended for your team.
6. Open the LarkID portal to check that the applications your manager requested are there.
7. Anything missing: ask your manager first, because access requests need their approval.

## Good to know

- The temporary access pass works once and expires 8 hours after it is issued.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Onboarding & Offboarding, New joiner. It is handled by Onboarding Team. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Make and receive phone calls in Teams

Desk phones have been replaced by calling in Teams; a direct number is assigned on request.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Open Teams and choose Calls; if there is no dial pad, you have no phone number yet.
2. Request a number in HelpHub under Request telephony; your manager approves.
3. Set voicemail and call forwarding under Settings, Calls.
4. Use a certified headset; laptop speakers cause echo for the other side.
5. Emergency calls from Teams report the address of your registered office, not your home.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Teams. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

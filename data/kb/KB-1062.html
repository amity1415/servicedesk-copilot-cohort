Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone

Report a lost or stolen device within 2 hours, at any time of day, so IT can lock it before data is exposed.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Call the service desk straight away; lost devices are handled 24 hours a day as priority 2.
  2. Say when and where you last had the device and whether it was switched on and unlocked.
  3. IT locks the device remotely. Wiping a device needs the approval of the IT duty manager.
  4. Change your LarkID password at https://id.larkhaven.example/reset.
  5. For theft, report it to the police and add the report number to the ticket within 5 business days.
  6. If the device is found, do not use it; return it to IT for checking.

Good to know

  • A lost phone that had LarkID Authenticator also needs the article Move LarkID Authenticator to a new phone.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Security, Lost device. It is handled by Security Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

Leaving the company: accounts and equipment

Access ends at 18:00 local time on your last working day and equipment must be returned within 5 business days.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Your manager raises the leaver request in HelpHub as soon as the leaving date is agreed.
  2. Move work files that colleagues need from your OneDrive to the team's shared folder before your last day.
  3. Remove personal files from the laptop; IT does not recover them afterwards.
  4. Unenrol your personal phone in DeviceCare on your last day.
  5. Return laptop, charger, dock, phone and badge at a Tech Bar, or use the prepaid box sent to home workers.
  6. Your mailbox is kept for 90 days and your manager can ask for access to it.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Onboarding & Offboarding, Leaver. It is handled by Onboarding Team. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

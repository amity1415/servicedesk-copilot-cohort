IT support hours and how to reach the service desk (Americas)

The Americas service desk is open 07:00 to 19:00 Central Time, Monday to Friday; priority 1 and lost devices are handled 24 hours a day.

Applies to: Any operating system | Americas | All employees

Key points

  • Raise and track tickets at https://helphub.larkhaven.example, at any time.
  • Call extension 4357 or +1 312 555 0143 when you cannot work at all, or to report a lost device or a security concern.
  • Walk-up help is at the Tech Bar: Chicago, floor 3, 09:00 to 16:00; Toronto, floor 2, 10:00 to 15:00.
  • Chat with the assistant in HelpHub for how-to questions; it can raise a ticket for you.
  • Outside support hours, tickets are picked up the next business day unless they are priority 1.

Good to know

  • Applies to offices in Americas.

Request access to a shared folder

Access to a departmental shared folder is approved by the folder owner, not by IT.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Ask a colleague for the exact folder path, for example \\files.larkhaven.example\finance\budgets.
  2. In HelpHub choose Request access, then Shared folder, and paste the path.
  3. Choose Read or Read and write.
  4. The folder owner receives the request and approves or declines it.
  5. After approval, sign out of your laptop and sign in again so the new permission applies.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Shared folder. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

Related policy: POL-002 Access Control Policy.

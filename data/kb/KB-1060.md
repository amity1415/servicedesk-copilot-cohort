# Laptop asks for a BitLocker recovery key

Windows asks for the 48-digit BitLocker recovery key after certain hardware or firmware changes; you can look the key up yourself.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- A blue screen at start-up asks you to enter the recovery key for this drive.

## Likely causes

- A firmware or BIOS update.
- The laptop was started with a new dock or USB device attached.
- Several wrong PIN attempts.

## Resolution

1. On your phone or another computer open https://id.larkhaven.example/devices and sign in.
2. Choose your laptop under My devices, then Show recovery key.
3. Check that the Key ID on the laptop screen matches the first eight characters shown in the portal.
4. Type the 48-digit key on the laptop.
5. If the laptop asks again at every start, raise a ticket; IT will suspend and resume BitLocker to reset it.

## Good to know

- If you cannot sign in to the portal, the service desk can read the key to you after verifying your identity.

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Encryption. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Remote work set-up checklist

What to check before your first day working away from the office.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Install SecureTunnel from App Hub and connect once while you are still in the office.
2. Register LarkID Authenticator; remote sign-in always needs it.
3. Take your charger and headset; docks and screens for home are requested in HelpHub.
4. Check your home connection gives at least 5 Mbps download.
5. Lock the screen whenever you step away and do not let family members use the laptop.
6. Do not print work documents at home; home printers are not supported.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under General Enquiry, Remote work. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Mailbox is full or nearly full

Mailboxes hold 50 GB; at 49 GB you are warned and at 50 GB you can no longer send.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. In Outlook open File, Tools, Mailbox Cleanup to see which folders are largest.
2. Sort the largest folder by size and delete or save big attachments to OneDrive.
3. Empty Deleted Items and Junk Email.
4. Mail older than 24 months moves to your Online Archive automatically; check it has not been turned off.
5. If you are still above 45 GB, raise a ticket to have the archive start at 12 months.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Mailbox. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

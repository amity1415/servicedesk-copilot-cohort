# SecureTunnel device check failed (error ST-407)

ST-407 means the laptop failed the security check that runs before every VPN connection.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- SecureTunnel shows 'Device does not meet requirements. ST-407' and lists the failed check.

## Likely causes

- Disk encryption is turned off or still encrypting.
- EndpointShield is not running or its definitions are older than 7 days.
- Operating system updates are more than 30 days behind.

## Resolution

1. Read the failed check named under the error message.
2. For Encryption, confirm BitLocker or FileVault is on and wait for encryption to finish.
3. For Endpoint protection, open EndpointShield and choose Update now, then restart.
4. For Updates, install all pending operating system updates and restart.
5. Connect again; the check runs each time you connect.

## Good to know

- Personal laptops always fail the device check. Use a company laptop or the web portal for email.

# Service desk: publish a service status update

The status page has three states, and only the incident manager or a level 2 analyst may publish to it.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. Operational: the service works normally.
2. Degraded: the service works but is slow or failing for some users.
3. Outage: the service is unavailable for an office, a region or everyone.
4. Choose the service and the region affected; choose Global only when all regions are affected.
5. Write what users will notice, not the technical cause.
6. Set the entry to Operational with a closing note when the incident ends; entries are kept for 90 days.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Service Desk L1 lead and record what you have already checked.

# Service desk: communication during a priority 1 incident

During a priority 1 incident the status page is updated within 15 minutes and then every 30 minutes until service is restored.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. The first analyst to confirm the incident pages the incident manager on duty.
2. Publish a status page entry within 15 minutes: what is affected, where, and the time of the next update.
3. Link every related ticket to the parent incident instead of working them one by one.
4. Update the status page every 30 minutes, even when there is nothing new.
5. On restoration, post the time and ask users to restart the affected application.
6. A review is published within 5 business days.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Service Desk L1 lead and record what you have already checked.

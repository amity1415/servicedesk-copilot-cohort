# Workplace Engineering: clear a stuck FollowMe print queue

When several users in one office cannot release jobs, the regional print server queue is usually stuck on one damaged job.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Symptoms

- Jobs appear at the printer but never print.
- Three or more tickets from one office within 30 minutes.

## Likely causes

- A damaged job at the head of the queue.
- The badge reader service has lost its connection to the directory.

## Resolution

1. Open the print management console for the region and sort the queue by submitted time.
2. Delete the oldest job in Error state.
3. If jobs still do not move, restart the Print Spooler service on the regional print server.
4. If badges are not recognised, restart the Badge Connector service instead.
5. Send a test page and release it at a printer before closing the tickets.

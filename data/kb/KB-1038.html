Outlook keeps asking for your password on macOS

Repeated password prompts in Outlook come from a saved old password or a sign-in session that expired.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • A password box appears every few minutes.
  • Typing the correct password makes the box come back.

Likely causes

  • The LarkID password was changed recently.
  • Outlook is signed in with a cached session that has expired.

Resolution

  1. Quit Outlook and Teams.
  2. Open Keychain Access, search for Exchange and for larkhaven.example, and delete the matching entries.
  3. Start Outlook and sign in once with LarkID, approving the prompt on your phone.
  4. If the prompt returns, open Outlook, Settings, Accounts, remove the account and add it again.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Outlook. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

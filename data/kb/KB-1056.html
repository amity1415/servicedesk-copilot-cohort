Laptop does not turn on or does not charge

A laptop that seems dead usually has a drained battery or a charger fault; a 30-second power reset fixes many cases.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • Nothing happens when you press the power button.
  • The charging light is off or blinks.

Likely causes

  • A fully drained battery.
  • A faulty charger, cable or dock.
  • The laptop is in a hung sleep state.

Resolution

  1. Connect the original charger directly to a wall socket, not through a dock, and wait 15 minutes.
  2. Try a different socket and, if you can, a colleague's charger of the same type.
  3. Hold the power button for 30 seconds, release it, then press it once.
  4. Disconnect docks, USB devices and external screens, then try again.
  5. If there is still no sign of power, raise a ticket or visit a Tech Bar; a loan laptop is issued while yours is repaired.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Power. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

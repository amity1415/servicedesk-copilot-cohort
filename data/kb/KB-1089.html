Travelling abroad with company devices

Tell IT before travelling to a high-risk country; you may be given a loan laptop and phone for the trip.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Check the high-risk country list on the intranet travel page.
  2. For a listed country, raise a HelpHub travel request at least 5 business days before departure.
  3. IT issues a clean loan laptop and phone; your own devices stay at home.
  4. Use only SecureTunnel on hotel, airport and conference Wi-Fi.
  5. Keep devices with you; do not leave them in hotel rooms or checked baggage.
  6. Return loan devices within 2 business days of coming back; they are wiped.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under General Enquiry, Travel. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

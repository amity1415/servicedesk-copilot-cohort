Print in the office with FollowMe printing (Asia Pacific)

All office printers share one queue, FollowMe-APAC; send the job, then release it at any printer with your badge.

Applies to: Any operating system | Asia Pacific | All employees

Steps

  1. Open App Hub and run Add office printers; the queue FollowMe-APAC is installed.
  2. Print to FollowMe-APAC from any application.
  3. At any printer, tap your badge on the reader and choose Print all or select jobs.
  4. Jobs not released within 24 hours are deleted.
  5. To scan, tap your badge, choose Scan to my email, and the scan arrives as a PDF.
  6. For paper jams or toner, use the QR code on the printer; for anything else raise a ticket. Support hours are 07:00 to 19:00 India Standard Time, Monday to Friday.

Good to know

  • Applies to offices in Asia Pacific. Home printers are not supported.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Printing. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Report a suspicious email

Use the Report phishing button for any suspicious email; if you clicked a link or typed your password, also call the service desk at once.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Select the message and choose Report phishing in the Outlook toolbar; the message moves out of your inbox.
3. If you already clicked a link or opened an attachment, call the service desk now; this is handled as priority 2.
4. If you typed your password on the page, change it at https://id.larkhaven.example/reset before doing anything else.
5. Security Operations replies within 1 hour to say whether the email was malicious.

## Good to know

- Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues or to the service desk; forwarding spreads the risk.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Security, Phishing. It is handled by Security Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

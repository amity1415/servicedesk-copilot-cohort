# Reset your LarkID password

Reset a forgotten LarkID password yourself in about two minutes, without calling the service desk.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Open https://id.larkhaven.example/reset in any browser, on any network.
2. Enter your work email address and choose Verify with LarkID Authenticator.
3. Approve the prompt on your phone and type the two-digit number shown on the screen.
4. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters that is not one of your last 10 passwords.
5. Lock and unlock your laptop with the new password so the saved sign-in is updated.
6. Update the password in the mail app on your phone if it asks for it.

## Good to know

- If you have no working authentication method, the service desk must verify your identity before resetting the password.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Password. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

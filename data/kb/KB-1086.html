What to do if you suspect malware

If a laptop shows signs of malware, take it off the network, leave it switched on and call the service desk.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • Files renamed or impossible to open.
  • Pop-ups demanding payment.
  • The mouse moving or windows opening by themselves.

Steps

  1. Disconnect from the network: turn off Wi-Fi and unplug the network cable and dock.
  2. Leave the laptop switched on; switching it off destroys evidence.
  3. Call the service desk from a phone; do not use the affected laptop to raise the ticket.
  4. Do not pay, click or type anything that the laptop demands.
  5. Security Operations isolates the laptop and tells you when it is safe to use.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Security, Malware. It is handled by Security Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

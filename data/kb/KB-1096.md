# IT support hours and how to reach the service desk (Europe, Middle East and Africa)

The Europe, Middle East and Africa service desk is open 07:00 to 19:00 UK time, Monday to Friday; priority 1 and lost devices are handled 24 hours a day.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | Europe, Middle East and Africa | All employees

## Key points

- Raise and track tickets at https://helphub.larkhaven.example, at any time.
- Call extension 4357 or +44 20 7946 0143 when you cannot work at all, or to report a lost device or a security concern.
- Walk-up help is at the Tech Bar: London, floor 5, 09:00 to 16:00; Munich, floor 1, 09:30 to 15:30.
- Chat with the assistant in HelpHub for how-to questions; it can raise a ticket for you.
- Outside support hours, tickets are picked up the next business day unless they are priority 1.

## Good to know

- Applies to offices in Europe, Middle East and Africa.

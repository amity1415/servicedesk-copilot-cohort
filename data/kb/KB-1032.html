Improve a slow home connection for remote work

Most slow remote sessions are caused by the home network; these checks fix the common cases before you raise a ticket.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Run a speed test without the VPN. Calls need at least 5 Mbps download and 2 Mbps upload.
  2. Move within one room of the router, or connect with a network cable.
  3. Use the 5 GHz band if your router offers two networks.
  4. Pause large downloads, backups and streaming on other devices during calls.
  5. Restart the router if it has been running for weeks.
  6. If the speed test is good without the VPN and poor with it, raise a ticket with both results.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, Home network. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

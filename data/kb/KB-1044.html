Camera or microphone does not work in Teams on macOS

Teams needs permission from the operating system to use the camera and microphone, and the right device must be selected.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • Others cannot hear or see you.
  • Teams shows a crossed-out camera or 'No microphone found'.

Likely causes

  • Privacy settings block the camera or microphone.
  • Teams is using the wrong device, such as a dock with no microphone.
  • Another app is using the camera.

Resolution

  1. In Teams open Settings, Devices, and choose the right speaker, microphone and camera; make a test call.
  2. Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Camera, and turn on Microsoft Teams. Do the same for Microphone.
  3. Quit and reopen Teams; macOS applies the permission only after a restart of the app.
  4. Close other apps that use the camera.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Teams. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

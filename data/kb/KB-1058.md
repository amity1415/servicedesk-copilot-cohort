# Set up a docking station and external screens on Windows

Connect screens to the dock, the dock to the laptop with the single USB-C cable, then arrange the screens in display settings.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Connect the dock to power and both screens to the dock.
2. Connect the dock's USB-C cable to the laptop port marked with a lightning or D symbol.
3. Press Windows key + P and choose Extend.
4. Open Settings, System, Display, drag the screens into the order they sit on your desk and choose the main display.
5. If a screen stays black, open App Hub and run Driver updates, then restart with the dock connected.

# Book a meeting room and use the room screen (Europe, Middle East and Africa)

Rooms are booked from Outlook and released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | Europe, Middle East and Africa | All employees

## Steps

1. In a new Outlook meeting choose Room Finder and pick a room in your building.
2. Add a Teams link if anyone joins remotely; room screens join with one touch.
3. At the start, tap Check in on the panel outside the room (RoomBoard London and RoomBoard Munich).
4. To present, tap Share on the table console, or join the meeting from your laptop and share there.
5. If the screen is blank, check the console is awake and the input is set to Room PC.
6. For a broken screen, camera or panel, raise a ticket with the room name. Support hours are 07:00 to 19:00 UK time, Monday to Friday.

## Good to know

- Applies to offices in Europe, Middle East and Africa.

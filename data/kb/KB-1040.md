# Share your calendar or give someone delegate access

You control who sees or manages your calendar; IT does not need to be involved.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. In Outlook open Calendar, right-click your calendar and choose Sharing Permissions.
2. Add the person and choose Can view all details, or Can edit.
3. For someone who sends invitations for you, choose Delegate and decide whether they see private items.
4. The other person accepts the sharing invitation that arrives by email.
5. Remove a person from the same screen when they no longer need access.

# Install and set up SecureTunnel VPN on Windows

SecureTunnel is the company VPN; install it from App Hub and sign in with LarkID to reach internal systems from outside the office.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Open App Hub from the Start menu and install SecureTunnel.
2. Start SecureTunnel from the system tray; the gateway vpn.larkhaven.example is filled in already.
3. Choose Connect and sign in with your LarkID email and password.
4. Approve the prompt in LarkID Authenticator.
5. Wait for the device check to finish; the tray icon turns green when you are connected.
6. Open https://intranet.larkhaven.example to confirm internal sites load.

## Good to know

- A VPN session ends after 12 hours or after 30 minutes without activity. Reconnect to continue.

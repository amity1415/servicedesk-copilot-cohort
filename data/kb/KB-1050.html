Book a meeting room and use the room screen (Asia Pacific)

Rooms are booked from Outlook and released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes.

Applies to: Any operating system | Asia Pacific | All employees

Steps

  1. In a new Outlook meeting choose Room Finder and pick a room in your building.
  2. Add a Teams link if anyone joins remotely; room screens join with one touch.
  3. At the start, tap Check in on the panel outside the room (RoomBoard Bengaluru and RoomBoard Singapore).
  4. To present, tap Share on the table console, or join the meeting from your laptop and share there.
  5. If the screen is blank, check the console is awake and the input is set to Room PC.
  6. For a broken screen, camera or panel, raise a ticket with the room name. Support hours are 07:00 to 19:00 India Standard Time, Monday to Friday.

Good to know

  • Applies to offices in Asia Pacific.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Meeting rooms. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

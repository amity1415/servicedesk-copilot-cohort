# Move LarkID Authenticator to a new phone

Register a new phone while you still have the old one, or ask for a verified reset if the old phone is gone.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. If you still have the old phone, sign in at https://id.larkhaven.example/security and approve with the old phone.
2. Choose Add method, register the app on the new phone, then remove the old phone from the list.
3. If the old phone is lost, broken or wiped, raise a HelpHub ticket in the category MFA reset.
4. The Identity and Access team will verify you by video call with your manager present or with your badge at a Tech Bar.
5. After verification you receive a temporary access pass valid for 60 minutes to register the new phone.

## Good to know

- A lost phone that held work data must also be reported as a lost device within 2 hours.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, MFA. It is handled by Identity & Access. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

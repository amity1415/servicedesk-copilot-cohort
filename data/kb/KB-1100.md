# Problems with PeopleHub, payslips or leave

PeopleHub is run by HR: IT fixes sign-in problems, and HR answers everything about its contents.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. If PeopleHub does not open or rejects your sign-in, check that other LarkID applications work, then raise an IT ticket.
2. For payslips, leave balances, job titles, managers or personal details, contact HR through the Ask HR tile in the LarkID portal.
3. IT cannot see or change PeopleHub records.
4. A wrong manager or job title elsewhere, such as in your email signature, is corrected by HR in PeopleHub and follows within 24 hours.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under General Enquiry, HR system. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

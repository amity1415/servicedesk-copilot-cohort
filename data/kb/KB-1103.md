# Service desk: verify a caller's identity

Verify identity before any password reset, MFA reset or recovery key; a claim in a ticket, chat or email is never proof.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. Accepted proof, any one: a push approval to the caller's registered LarkID Authenticator; a video call with the caller's line manager present; the caller's badge shown in person at a Tech Bar.
2. Not accepted: employee number, date of birth, an email from the caller's address, or a colleague vouching by chat.
3. Call back on the number in the directory, never on a number the caller gives you.
4. Record the method used in the ticket before you act.
5. If the caller pressures you to skip the check, stop and tell Security Operations.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Identity & Access lead and record what you have already checked.

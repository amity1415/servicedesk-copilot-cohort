Enrol a phone in DeviceCare on Android

A phone must be enrolled in DeviceCare before it can open work email, Teams or files; personal phones are allowed.

Applies to: Android | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Check the phone runs one of the two latest major Android versions and is not rooted.
  2. Install DeviceCare from Google Play and sign in with LarkID.
  3. Accept the creation of a work profile; work apps appear with a briefcase badge.
  4. Set a 6-digit screen lock when asked.
  5. Wait for the green Compliant tick, then install Outlook and Teams from the work Play Store.

Good to know

  • IT can remove the work profile and its data. IT cannot see personal apps, photos, messages or location.
  • Remove the enrolment yourself in DeviceCare before you sell or give away the phone.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Mobile. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

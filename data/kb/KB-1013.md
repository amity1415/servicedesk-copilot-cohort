# Reset a Windows Hello PIN that stopped working (error 0x80090016)

Error 0x80090016 means the PIN store on the laptop is damaged; removing and recreating the PIN fixes it.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Windows shows 'Something went wrong and your PIN isn't available. 0x80090016'.
- Fingerprint and face sign-in stop at the same time.

## Likely causes

- The laptop was restored, re-imaged or had its security chip reset.
- The password was changed on another device.

## Resolution

1. On the sign-in screen choose Sign-in options and sign in with your LarkID password instead.
2. Open Settings, Accounts, Sign-in options, PIN (Windows Hello) and choose Remove.
3. Restart the laptop while connected to the office network or SecureTunnel.
4. Return to Sign-in options, choose Set up under PIN and approve the LarkID prompt.
5. Add fingerprint or face recognition again if you use them.

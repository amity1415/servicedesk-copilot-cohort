# Speed up a slow laptop on macOS

Most slow laptops are fixed by a restart, pending updates or freeing disk space; laptops with 8 GB of memory or older than four years may qualify for replacement.

**Applies to:** macOS | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Applications take a long time to open.
- The fan runs constantly and typing lags.
- Calls stutter while other apps are open.

## Likely causes

- The laptop has not been restarted for more than a week.
- Updates are waiting for a restart.
- Less than 15 percent of the disk is free.
- Too many apps start automatically.
- 8 GB of memory is not enough for the applications in use.

## Resolution

1. Restart the Mac from the Apple menu.
2. Open System Settings, General, Software Update, install what is offered and restart again.
3. Open Activity Monitor and sort by Memory and by CPU. Quit what you do not need.
4. Open System Settings, General, Login Items, and remove apps you do not use daily.
5. Open System Settings, General, Storage, and free space until at least 15 percent of the disk is free.
6. If Memory Pressure in Activity Monitor stays red with only your normal apps open, raise a ticket; include the laptop asset tag.

## Good to know

- A regional service incident can also make everything feel slow. Check the service status page in HelpHub first.
- Laptops are replaced after 48 months. A laptop with 8 GB of memory can be replaced earlier when IT confirms the memory is the cause.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Performance. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

Send a file that is too large for email

Email attachments are limited to 25 MB; use a OneDrive link for colleagues and SendSafe for people outside the company.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. For colleagues, upload the file to OneDrive, choose Share and send the link.
  2. For external recipients, open https://sendsafe.larkhaven.example and sign in with LarkID.
  3. Upload up to 20 GB, enter the recipient's email address and set an expiry of at most 14 days.
  4. The recipient gets a link and a one-time code by separate emails.
  5. Files classified Restricted must not be sent through SendSafe without the data owner's written approval.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, File transfer. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

Related policy: POL-007 Data Classification and Handling Policy.

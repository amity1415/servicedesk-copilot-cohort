Recover a deleted email or file

Deleted email can be recovered for 30 days and deleted OneDrive files for 93 days, without a ticket.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Email: open Deleted Items and move the message back to your inbox.
  2. If it is not there, choose Recover items recently removed from this folder at the top of Deleted Items.
  3. Files: open OneDrive in a browser, choose Recycle bin, select the file and choose Restore.
  4. For an earlier version of a file, right-click it in OneDrive and choose Version history.
  5. After these periods the item cannot be recovered by IT either.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Recovery. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

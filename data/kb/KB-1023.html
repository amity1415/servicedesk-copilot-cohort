VPN keeps disconnecting on macOS

Frequent VPN drops are usually caused by weak Wi-Fi or power saving on the network adapter, not by the VPN service.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Symptoms

  • The VPN drops every few minutes.
  • Calls freeze and SecureTunnel shows Reconnecting.

Likely causes

  • Weak or congested Wi-Fi.
  • The network adapter sleeps to save power.
  • An out-of-date SecureTunnel client.

Resolution

  1. Check the service status page in HelpHub for a VPN incident in your region.
  2. Move closer to the router or use a network cable, then test again.
  3. Open System Settings, Wi-Fi, Details for your network, and turn off Limit IP address tracking.
  4. Update SecureTunnel from App Hub to version 5.3 or later; version 5.2 drops on macOS 26.
  5. If drops continue on a wired connection, raise a ticket with the times of three recent drops.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, VPN. It is handled by Network Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

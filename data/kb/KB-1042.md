# Teams sign-in fails with error caa70004

Error caa70004 appears when Teams on Windows cannot complete sign-in, usually because of a damaged cache.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Teams shows 'We're sorry, we've run into an issue' with error code caa70004.
- Teams in the browser works.

## Likely causes

- A damaged Teams cache.
- The laptop date or time is wrong.
- Another work account is signed in to Windows.

## Resolution

1. Check that Teams works at https://teams.microsoft.com in a browser.
2. Right-click the Teams icon in the system tray and choose Quit.
3. Open Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Microsoft Teams, Advanced options, and choose Reset.
4. Check the date, time and time zone are set automatically.
5. Start Teams and sign in with LarkID.

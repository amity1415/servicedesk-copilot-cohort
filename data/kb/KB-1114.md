# Security Operations: lock or wipe a lost device

Any analyst may lock a lost device at once; a full wipe destroys data and needs the IT duty manager's approval.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. Lock the device immediately from the management console; no approval is needed.
2. Revoke the user's sign-in sessions and have them change their password.
3. For a personal phone, remove the work profile only; never wipe a personal device completely.
4. For a company laptop or phone, request a full wipe from the IT duty manager and record the approval in the ticket.
5. Send the wipe command only after approval; it runs when the device next comes online.
6. If the device is recovered, re-image it before reuse.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Security Operations lead and record what you have already checked.

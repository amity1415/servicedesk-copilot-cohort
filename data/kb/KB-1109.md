# Workplace Engineering: repair or re-image a laptop

Stop troubleshooting a software fault after 2 hours of analyst time and re-image; the user's data is in OneDrive.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | IT staff only

## Steps

1. Confirm OneDrive shows Up to date and that Desktop, Documents and Pictures are backed up.
2. Ask about data outside those folders, such as local databases or virtual machines.
3. Re-image when: the fault survives a clean user profile, system file checks fail, or 2 hours have been spent.
4. Replace instead when the laptop is older than 48 months, or hardware diagnostics fail.
5. Issue a loan laptop if the re-image cannot be finished the same day.
6. After the re-image, check encryption, EndpointShield and the device certificate before returning it.

## Still not working?

Escalate to the Workplace Engineering lead and record what you have already checked.

Free up disk space on Windows

Keep at least 15 percent of the disk free; OneDrive can keep files online only so they stop using local space.

Applies to: Windows | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Open Settings, System, Storage, to see what is using space.
  2. Turn on Storage Sense and choose Run Storage Sense now.
  3. Right-click the OneDrive folder in File Explorer and choose Free up space.
  4. Empty the Recycle Bin and the Downloads folder.
  5. Uninstall applications you no longer use from Settings, Apps.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Storage. It is handled by Service Desk L1. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

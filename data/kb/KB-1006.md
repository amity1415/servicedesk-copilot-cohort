# Fix rejected approval codes in LarkID Authenticator (error LID-MFA-102)

Error LID-MFA-102 means the code from the phone does not match the server, almost always because the phone clock is wrong.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Sign-in shows 'Verification failed. LID-MFA-102'.
- Six-digit codes are rejected although they are typed correctly.

## Likely causes

- The phone date, time or time zone is set manually.
- The account was re-registered and the old entry is still in the app.

## Resolution

1. On the phone, set date and time to automatic and confirm the time zone is correct.
2. Close and reopen LarkID Authenticator, then try a new code.
3. If there are two Larkhaven entries in the app, delete the older one.
4. Use a push approval instead of typing a code if the option is offered.
5. If codes are still rejected, raise a ticket; the Identity and Access team will reset your registration after verifying your identity.

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, MFA. It is handled by Identity & Access. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

# Mac does not accept your password at start-up (FileVault)

If the FileVault unlock screen rejects your password, use the recovery key stored in the LarkID portal to unlock the Mac and set the password again.

**Applies to:** macOS | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- The first login screen after a restart rejects the current password.
- A question mark appears after three attempts.

## Likely causes

- The LarkID password was changed while the Mac was switched off or away from the network.

## Resolution

1. Try the previous password; the start-up screen may still expect it.
2. On your phone open https://id.larkhaven.example/devices, choose the Mac and Show recovery key.
3. On the Mac click the question mark, choose Restart and show password reset options, and enter the recovery key.
4. Set the Mac password to match your current LarkID password.
5. After signing in, connect to the office network or SecureTunnel so the passwords stay in step.

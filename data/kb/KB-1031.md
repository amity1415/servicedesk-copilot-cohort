# Give a visitor access to guest Wi-Fi (Asia Pacific)

Visitors use the guest network LarkGuest-APAC; a Larkhaven employee must sponsor each visitor.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | Asia Pacific | All employees

## Steps

1. Ask the visitor to join the network LarkGuest-APAC and open any web page.
2. On the sign-in page the visitor enters their name and your work email address as sponsor.
3. You receive an email; choose Approve. Access lasts until midnight local time.
4. For visits longer than one day, request a multi-day guest pass in HelpHub at least one business day ahead.
5. Guest Wi-Fi gives internet access only. Visitors cannot reach printers or internal systems.

## Good to know

- Applies to offices in Asia Pacific. Reception can sponsor visitors for all-hands events.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, Wi-Fi. It is handled by Network Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

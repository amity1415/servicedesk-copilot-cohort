# LarkNet stops connecting because the Wi-Fi certificate expired on Windows

The LarkNet certificate renews itself every 12 months; a laptop that was off the network at renewal time cannot join until it is repaired.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- LarkNet shows 'Cannot connect to this network' or 'Authentication failed'.
- The laptop worked on LarkNet until recently and other people nearby are connected.

## Likely causes

- The laptop was away from the office or VPN for more than 30 days around the renewal date.

## Resolution

1. Connect by cable, or join the guest network and start SecureTunnel.
2. Open App Hub and run Repair network certificate.
3. Open Settings, Network and internet, Wi-Fi, Manage known networks, and forget LarkNet.
4. Restart and select LarkNet again.

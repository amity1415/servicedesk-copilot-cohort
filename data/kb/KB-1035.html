Set up work email on your phone on Android

Work email on a phone needs the phone enrolled in DeviceCare and the Outlook app; the built-in mail app is blocked.

Applies to: Android | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Install DeviceCare from Google Play and sign in with LarkID to create the work profile.
  2. Open the work profile's Play Store, marked with a briefcase, and install Outlook.
  3. Open the work copy of Outlook, add your work address and sign in with LarkID.
  4. Set a 6-digit screen lock if the phone asks; patterns are not accepted.

Good to know

  • Enrolment lets IT remove work data from the phone. It does not show IT your photos, messages or personal apps.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Mobile email. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

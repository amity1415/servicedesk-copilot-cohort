# Certificate warning NET::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID on internal sites

Internal sites use certificates issued by Larkhaven; the warning appears when the company root certificate is missing from the device.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Chrome or Edge shows 'Your connection is not private' with NET::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID on an internal address.

## Likely causes

- The laptop is not managed or missed a certificate update.
- The date on the laptop is wrong.
- You are on a personal device.

## Resolution

1. Check that the address ends in larkhaven.example. If it does not, close the page and report it as phishing.
2. Check the laptop date and time are correct.
3. Open App Hub and run Repair network certificate, then restart the browser.
4. Do not choose Proceed anyway on a page that asks for your password.
5. If the warning remains on a company laptop, raise a ticket with the full address.

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, Certificates. It is handled by Network Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

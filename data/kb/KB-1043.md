# Camera or microphone does not work in Teams on Windows

Teams needs permission from the operating system to use the camera and microphone, and the right device must be selected.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Others cannot hear or see you.
- Teams shows a crossed-out camera or 'No microphone found'.

## Likely causes

- Privacy settings block the camera or microphone.
- Teams is using the wrong device, such as a dock with no microphone.
- Another app is using the camera.

## Resolution

1. In Teams open Settings, Devices, and choose the right speaker, microphone and camera; make a test call.
2. Open Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Camera, and turn on Let desktop apps access your camera. Do the same for Microphone.
3. Close other apps that use the camera, such as Zoom or the Camera app.
4. Unplug and reconnect a USB headset or dock.
5. Slide open the privacy shutter above the screen if the picture is black.

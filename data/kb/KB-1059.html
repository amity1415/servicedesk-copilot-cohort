Set up a docking station and external screens on macOS

Connect screens to the dock, the dock to the laptop with the single USB-C cable, then arrange the screens in display settings.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Connect the dock to power and the screens to the dock.
  2. Connect the dock's USB-C cable to any port on the Mac.
  3. Open System Settings, Displays, choose Arrange and drag the screens into the order they sit on your desk.
  4. MacBook Air models drive one external screen through a standard dock; a second screen needs a DisplayLink dock from IT.
  5. If a screen stays black, unplug the dock from power for 10 seconds and reconnect.

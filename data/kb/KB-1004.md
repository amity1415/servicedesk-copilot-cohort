# Set up LarkID Authenticator on iPhone and iPad

Install LarkID Authenticator on your phone and register it as your sign-in approval method.

**Applies to:** iPhone and iPad | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Install LarkID Authenticator from the App Store.
2. On your laptop, open https://id.larkhaven.example/security and choose Add method, then Authenticator app.
3. In the app, tap Add account and scan the QR code shown on the laptop.
4. Allow notifications when iOS asks; approvals arrive as notifications.
5. Approve the test prompt and type the two-digit number to finish.

## Good to know

- Text-message codes are not an approved method. A FIDO2 security key can be registered as a second method.

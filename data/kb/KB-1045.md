# Screen sharing shows a black screen on macOS

On a Mac, meeting apps can share the screen only after they are allowed under Screen Recording in System Settings.

**Applies to:** macOS | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- Participants see a black or frozen window when you share.
- Only the desktop background is shared, without windows.

## Likely causes

- Screen recording permission was never granted or was reset by a macOS update.

## Resolution

1. Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Screen and System Audio Recording.
2. Turn on Microsoft Teams, and your browser if you join meetings in the browser.
3. Choose Quit and Reopen when macOS offers it.
4. Start a test meeting and share a single window first.

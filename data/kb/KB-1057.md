# Request a replacement laptop

Laptops are replaced every 48 months; earlier replacement needs a confirmed fault or a role that needs a higher specification.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Check the age of your laptop at https://id.larkhaven.example/devices under My devices.
2. If it is 48 months or older, choose Request replacement in HelpHub; no approval is needed.
3. If it is younger, raise a ticket describing the problem; IT confirms whether repair or replacement is right.
4. Roles that need more than the standard specification need the manager's approval and a cost centre.
5. Back up nothing by hand: files in OneDrive and settings are restored on the new laptop.
6. Return the old laptop within 5 business days of receiving the new one.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Hardware & Devices, Hardware request. It is handled by Workplace Engineering. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

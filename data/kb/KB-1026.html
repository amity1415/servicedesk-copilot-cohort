Connect to LarkNet, the office Wi-Fi on macOS

Company laptops join LarkNet automatically with a certificate; you never type a Wi-Fi password.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar and choose LarkNet.
  2. If macOS asks for a user name and password, cancel; the certificate is missing.
  3. Connect a network cable or use the LarkGuest network with VPN, then open App Hub and run Repair network certificate.
  4. Restart and choose LarkNet again; approve the certificate prompt once with your Mac password.

Good to know

  • Phones and personal devices cannot join LarkNet. Use the guest network instead.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Network & VPN, Wi-Fi. It is handled by Network Operations. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

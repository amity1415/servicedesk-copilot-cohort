# Print in the office with FollowMe printing (Americas)

All office printers share one queue, FollowMe-AMER; send the job, then release it at any printer with your badge.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | Americas | All employees

## Steps

1. Open App Hub and run Add office printers; the queue FollowMe-AMER is installed.
2. Print to FollowMe-AMER from any application.
3. At any printer, tap your badge on the reader and choose Print all or select jobs.
4. Jobs not released within 24 hours are deleted.
5. To scan, tap your badge, choose Scan to my email, and the scan arrives as a PDF.
6. For paper jams or toner, use the QR code on the printer; for anything else raise a ticket. Support hours are 07:00 to 19:00 Central Time, Monday to Friday.

## Good to know

- Applies to offices in Americas. Home printers are not supported.

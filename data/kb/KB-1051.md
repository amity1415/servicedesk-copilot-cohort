# Set an out-of-office reply and the standard email signature

Set automatic replies before leave and use the standard signature generated from your HR record.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. In Outlook choose File, Automatic Replies, and set the dates of your absence.
2. Write one reply for colleagues and one for people outside the company; do not include personal phone numbers.
3. For the signature, open https://intranet.larkhaven.example/signature and choose Copy signature.
4. In Outlook open Settings, Signatures, paste it and set it for new messages.
5. If your title or office is wrong in the signature, ask HR to correct your PeopleHub record; IT cannot edit it.

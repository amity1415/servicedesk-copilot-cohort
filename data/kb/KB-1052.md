# Create or change an email distribution list

Distribution lists are managed by their owners; IT creates new lists on request within 2 business days.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. To join or leave a list, ask its owner. The owner is shown on the list's contact card in Outlook.
2. Owners add and remove members at https://id.larkhaven.example/groups.
3. For a new list, raise a HelpHub request with the name, two owners and the purpose.
4. List addresses start with dl-, for example dl-sales-emea@larkhaven.example.
5. Lists with no owner for 30 days are disabled.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Email & Collaboration, Distribution list. It is handled by Messaging & Collaboration. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

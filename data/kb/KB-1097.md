# IT support hours and how to reach the service desk (Asia Pacific)

The Asia Pacific service desk is open 07:00 to 19:00 India Standard Time, Monday to Friday; priority 1 and lost devices are handled 24 hours a day.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | Asia Pacific | All employees

## Key points

- Raise and track tickets at https://helphub.larkhaven.example, at any time.
- Call extension 4357 or +65 6555 0143 when you cannot work at all, or to report a lost device or a security concern.
- Walk-up help is at the Tech Bar: Bengaluru, floor 4, 09:30 to 17:00; Singapore, floor 9, 10:00 to 15:00.
- Chat with the assistant in HelpHub for how-to questions; it can raise a ticket for you.
- Outside support hours, tickets are picked up the next business day unless they are priority 1.

## Good to know

- Applies to offices in Asia Pacific.

# Fix VPN error 809 on Windows

Error 809 means the network you are on blocks the ports the VPN needs; it is a problem with the local network, not your account.

**Applies to:** Windows | All regions | All employees

## Symptoms

- SecureTunnel shows 'The network connection between your computer and the VPN server could not be established. Error 809'.
- The VPN works on one network, such as a phone hotspot, but not on another.

## Likely causes

- A home router, hotel or guest network blocks UDP ports 500 and 4500.
- The router has IPsec passthrough turned off.
- A third-party firewall on the laptop blocks the VPN.

## Resolution

1. Connect the laptop to a phone hotspot and try the VPN. If it works, the first network is blocking it.
2. Restart the home router, then try again.
3. In the router settings turn on IPsec passthrough, sometimes called VPN passthrough.
4. In SecureTunnel open Settings, Protocol, and choose TLS fallback, which uses TCP port 443.
5. Restart the laptop and connect again.
6. If error 809 appears on every network, raise a ticket for Network Operations and attach the SecureTunnel log bundle.

## Good to know

- Hotels and public Wi-Fi often block VPN ports. TLS fallback is slower but works on almost every network.

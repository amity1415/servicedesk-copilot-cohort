Request licensed software

Licensed software is requested in HelpHub; the approvals needed depend on the annual charge shown in the software catalogue.

Applies to: Any operating system | All regions | All employees

Steps

  1. Look up the application in the software catalogue to see its annual charge and approval rule.
  2. In HelpHub choose Request software, pick the application and give the business reason.
  3. Charges under USD 300 a year need your manager's approval.
  4. Charges of USD 300 a year or more need your manager and your cost-centre owner.
  5. Applications that handle Restricted data also need the data owner.
  6. After the last approval the application appears in App Hub within 60 minutes.
  7. A licence not used for 60 days is reclaimed; you are warned by email 7 days before.

Good to know

  • Software that is not in the catalogue needs a security and legal review, which takes up to 10 business days.

Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Software, Licence request. It is handled by Software Packaging. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

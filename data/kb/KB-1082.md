# You received an 'Unapproved software detected' notice

The notice means software that is not in the catalogue was found on your laptop; remove it or ask for it to be reviewed within 5 business days.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Read the notice for the name of the application.
2. If you do not need it, uninstall it; the notice closes at the next daily scan.
3. If you need it for work, reply to the notice with the business reason; a review takes up to 10 business days.
4. Personal licences, trial versions and software from home must not be installed on company laptops.
5. After 5 business days with no action the application is removed automatically.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Software, Compliance. It is handled by Software Packaging. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

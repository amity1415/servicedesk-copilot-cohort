# Install and set up SecureTunnel VPN on macOS

SecureTunnel is the company VPN; install it from App Hub and sign in with LarkID to reach internal systems from outside the office.

Applies to: macOS | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Open App Hub from Launchpad and install SecureTunnel.

2. When macOS asks, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and allow the SecureTunnel system extension.

3. Allow SecureTunnel to add VPN configurations when prompted.

4. Click the SecureTunnel icon in the menu bar, choose Connect and sign in with LarkID.

5. Approve the prompt in LarkID Authenticator; the menu bar icon shows a closed lock when connected.

6. Open https://intranet.larkhaven.example to confirm internal sites load.

## Good to know

- A VPN session ends after 12 hours or after 30 minutes without activity. Reconnect to continue.

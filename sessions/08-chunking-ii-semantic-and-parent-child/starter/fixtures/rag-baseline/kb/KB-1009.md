# Request access to an application

Request application access in HelpHub; standard applications need your manager's approval and restricted ones also need the data owner's.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Open https://helphub.larkhaven.example and choose Request access.
2. Search for the application and pick the role you need; choose the lowest role that lets you do your job.
3. Write the business reason in one or two sentences. Requests without a reason are returned.
4. Submit. Your line manager is asked to approve; restricted applications are then sent to the data owner.
5. When approved, access is granted automatically within 60 minutes and the tile appears in the LarkID portal.

## Good to know

- Restricted applications include FinSight, CoreERP and PeopleHub administration.
- Nobody can approve their own request, and the service desk cannot approve on a manager's behalf.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Access & Identity, Application access. It is handled by Identity & Access. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.

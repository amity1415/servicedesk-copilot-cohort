# Managers: request IT setup for a new joiner

Submit the new joiner form at least 5 business days before the start date; IT can begin only after HR has created the PeopleHub record.

**Applies to:** Any operating system | All regions | All employees

## Steps

1. Confirm with HR that the joiner's PeopleHub record exists and shows the correct start date; IT cannot create an account without it.
2. In HelpHub choose New joiner and select the person from the PeopleHub list.
3. Choose the laptop: Standard, or Developer with a business reason.
4. Choose the role profile for the team; it sets the usual applications and access groups.
5. Add anything beyond the profile as separate access requests; restricted applications still need the data owner.
6. Say where the laptop goes: a Tech Bar for collection or a home address.
7. Two business days before the start date you receive the temporary access pass to hand over on day one.

## Good to know

- Requests made less than 5 business days ahead are handled as best effort and the laptop may be a loan device.
- Access is set from the role profile, never by copying another person's access.

## Still not working?

Raise a ticket in HelpHub under Onboarding & Offboarding, New joiner. It is handled by Onboarding Team. Include the exact error message and your asset tag.
